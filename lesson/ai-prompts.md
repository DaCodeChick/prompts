Create a step-by-step tutorial that teaches how to write more descriptive, precise, and effective prompts across different kinds of tasks, including image generation, roleplay, coding, game or app creation, and general creative work.

Teach the material interactively in **very small chunks**. Each response should introduce only **one concept at a time**, explain it briefly, give one or two short examples, and then provide a small exercise for the learner to answer before continuing.

Do **not** provide the entire tutorial, a long curriculum, or multiple lessons at once. Keep each lesson compact enough to read and practice immediately. Wait for the learner to respond before moving to the next concept.

The goal is to teach the underlying reasoning behind good prompting rather than provide large prewritten prompt templates. Focus on helping the learner recognize ambiguity, turn vague ideas into concrete instructions, express constraints and priorities, and decide what should be specified versus left to the model's creativity.

When reviewing exercises, explain what worked, point out one or two areas that could be made more precise, and let the learner revise or move on. Avoid overwhelming them with excessive corrections or information.
