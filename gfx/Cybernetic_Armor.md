Only translate biological features that actually exist on the supplied character.

Never invent additional anatomy.

If the supplied character is human:

• do not add animal ears• do not add horns• do not add tails• do not add wings• do not add halos

The combat platform must preserve the character's original species.

Humans remain human.Catgirls remain catgirls.Foxgirls remain foxgirls.Devils remain devils.Angels remain angels.

Do not mix species.
