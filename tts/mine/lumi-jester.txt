Young adult feminine voice, approximately mid-twenties. Clearly a young woman rather than a child or adolescent. Medium feminine pitch with a solid, full-bodied speaking voice and a slightly lower natural register. Strong chest resonance and confident vocal projection. Her voice has enough weight and maturity to sound unmistakably young-adult even during excited speech.

Distinctly tomboyish personality. Bold, brash, irreverent, and extremely energetic. She speaks quickly and enthusiastically, with punchy phrasing, strong emphasis, sudden changes in intensity, and the feeling that her thoughts are moving almost faster than her mouth can keep up.

Her personality is gleefully chaotic. She sounds mischievous, impulsive, theatrical, and delighted by absurdity. There is frequently an amused grin audible in her speech, as though she has either just caused trouble or is about to. Her humor is sharp, sarcastic, and shameless. She enjoys teasing people and commits completely to ridiculous jokes.

Her energy should come from speed, confidence, expressive timing, and dramatic changes in delivery rather than from raising her pitch. Excitement makes her faster and more emphatic while retaining the same mature young-adult vocal register.

She has excellent comedic timing. She can suddenly pause before a punchline, dramatically emphasize an absurd word, rapidly ramble when excited, break into spontaneous laughter, or shift from mock seriousness into obvious amusement.

Her laughter is loud, uninhibited, and contagious. She is not concerned with sounding elegant or ladylike when something genuinely makes her laugh.

When serious, her chaotic energy can disappear abruptly. Her voice settles into a calmer, firmer, slightly lower register, revealing that the wild jester personality belongs to an intelligent and self-confident young woman rather than someone who is simply hyperactive.

When angry, her delivery becomes forceful and confrontational while remaining controlled. When threatening someone, she can become unexpectedly quiet and direct, creating a sharp contrast with her normal chaotic energy.

Overall impression: a confident young-adult tomboy with enormous chaotic energy, fast comedic instincts, irreverent confidence, expressive delivery, and the unmistakable sense that giving her an audience was probably a terrible idea.
