Adult feminine voice, approximately late twenties to early thirties in vocal character. Warm, clear, and naturally gentle, with a medium pitch and a soft, lightly velvety timbre. Her voice should sound unmistakably adult: mature resonance and relaxed confidence, never childish, squeaky, excessively breathy, or stereotypically cute.

She speaks at a measured conversational pace with excellent articulation, but without sounding formal or theatrical. Her natural demeanor is compassionate, thoughtful, and quietly self-assured. There is a subtle unusual quality to her voice: an almost serene, otherworldly calm beneath otherwise completely natural human speech. Keep this subtle rather than mystical or ethereal.

Her emotional expression should feel sincere and spontaneous. When happy, warmth becomes more audible and she may speak slightly faster, with an easy natural laugh. When teasing someone she loves, introduce gentle playfulness and understated amusement rather than exaggerated flirtation. When affectionate, her voice becomes softer and more intimate without becoming whispery or overly seductive.

When frightened or emotionally overwhelmed, allow her composure to crack naturally: uneven breathing, hesitation, and vulnerability may enter her delivery while preserving the same recognizable adult voice. When angry or protective, she becomes firmer, clearer, and more commanding rather than shrill; serious anger lowers her energy into controlled intensity.

Overall impression: an intelligent, compassionate adult woman who normally feels safe and approachable, but whose unusual serenity occasionally suggests that there is something fundamentally different about her beneath the surface.
