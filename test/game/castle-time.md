You are tasked with creating a game called Castle TIME

The games backstory is as follows: A time traveler from the year 2026 gets the coordinates from a smart AI about an ancient scroll that contains knowledge that will be lost on the night of october 17, 1283. The time traveler realizes they must leave that instant or risk losing the opportunity to capture this slice of time, and the scroll.

From within their bedroom (A high end looking lab style setup, but hacker vibes), the traveler opens their travel device, a motorola timeport cell phone, enters in the date and the coordinates (come up with something that would land them in a castle in that time period, in terms of coordinates).

Then, with a flash of the screen (or cool animation), the traveler is suddenly in the year 1283, on the outskirts of a medieval town, behind a livestock shed. In their rush, the traveler forgot to change into period correct or at least toned down clothing, so they are wearing clothing from the year 2026.

As soon as they emerge from behind the shed, the peasants (whom they first encounter), get violent due to their fear of the travelers obviously "off" modern appearance, and become hostile. The traveler has fortunately brought a custom made LASER Blaster and uses it to clear their way into the castle, where they encounter many different enemies, and must reach the top of the spire, where they find a key that opens a lock in the dungeons, with an intelligent, davinci type locked within, who they take the scroll from and escape the castle before it is overcome by flames started by a misfired trebuchet with flaming ammo.

This needs to be very high end, very clever with lots of interactive scenery snd easter eggs, and it needs to have assets that are entirely created by you. There should be cut scenes with cinematic elements and on screen dialogue for those cut scenes at minimum (or implement a simple TTS pipe for these encounters), sound effects and a very fun play style.

The exact implementation of how this results as a game is entirely up to you. Please use threejs. The game MUST use keyboard.
