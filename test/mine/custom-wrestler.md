Create a polished **3D wrestling game** at the level of quality expected from a high-end indie game. The emphasis should be on responsive gameplay, expressive character presentation, environmental detail, and a fun, chaotic free-play wrestling experience.

## CHARACTER REFERENCES

I may provide **between zero and four character reference sheets/images** with this prompt.

Treat every supplied character reference as a wrestler in the game's roster.

* If **4 character references** are supplied, use those four characters as the complete roster.
* If **3 references** are supplied, use those three characters and procedurally create 1 additional wrestler.
* If **2 references** are supplied, use those two characters and procedurally create 2 additional wrestlers.
* If **1 reference** is supplied, use that character and procedurally create 3 additional wrestlers.
* If **no references** are supplied, procedurally create all 4 wrestlers.

There must **always be exactly four selectable wrestlers**.

For supplied characters, preserve their recognizable identity as closely as possible, including:

* Face and facial features
* Hair style and color
* Body proportions
* Skin tone
* Species or other distinctive physical traits
* Color palette
* Clothing motifs
* Accessories
* Overall personality and visual identity

Adapt their clothing into **functional professional wrestling attire inspired by their original design** rather than replacing their identity with generic wrestling clothing. The result should look like that specific character deliberately designed a wrestling outfit for themselves.

Do not unnecessarily alter a supplied character's gender, species, body type, age presentation, or other defining visual characteristics.

Procedurally generated wrestlers should likewise have distinctive identities, silhouettes, attire, personalities, wrestling styles, and themes rather than appearing like generic placeholder characters.

## START SCREEN

The game begins on a polished title/start screen featuring the wrestling ring and arena rendered fully in 3D.

The camera should slowly orbit or dramatically move around the ring while the menu is overlaid on the scene.

Include:

* Start / Enter the Ring
* Wrestler selection
* Music On/Off toggle
* Procedurally generated menu music
* Animated lighting and arena effects
* Attractive transitions between menus
* Character previews for all four wrestlers

The start screen must be **fully functional**. All buttons and wrestler-selection controls must actually work and transition correctly into gameplay.

## CHARACTER SELECTION

Before beginning the match, the player selects one of the four wrestlers.

Show a clear 3D preview of each wrestler and make their differences immediately recognizable.

The selected wrestler is extremely important because **their identity determines the procedural theme of the match arena**.

## PROCEDURAL CHARACTER-THEMED ARENA

Once the player chooses a wrestler, procedurally theme the wrestling arena around that character.

Analyze the selected character's visual design, personality, motifs, colors, apparent abilities, occupation/archetype, species, clothing, accessories, and overall aesthetic.

Use those characteristics as inspiration for:

* Arena architecture
* Wrestling ring design
* Ring ropes and turnbuckles
* Entrance ramp
* Lighting
* Audience presentation
* Banners and decorations
* Animated displays
* Environmental effects
* Music
* Sound effects
* Interactive objects
* Weapons and props
* Background scenery
* Match atmosphere

For example, a regal fantasy character might produce an elaborate palace-like arena with banners, ornate architecture, magical lighting, and ceremonial props, while a futuristic character might produce a neon technological arena with holographic displays and electronic effects.

These are examples only. **Infer an appropriate theme dynamically from whichever character the player selects.**

The transformation should be substantial. Do not merely recolor the same arena. Different character themes should feel like meaningfully different venues while retaining the basic requirements of a functional wrestling ring.

Props should likewise be character-themed whenever practical. Rather than relying entirely on generic folding chairs and tables, invent humorous or appropriate wrestling props based on the selected character.

## GAMEPLAY

When the match begins, place the player's selected wrestler inside the ring along with **two AI-controlled opponents chosen from the remaining roster**.

The fourth wrestler may remain outside the active match, appear as a spectator/manager, enter later, or otherwise be incorporated creatively if desired.

Gameplay should be responsive and immediately understandable.

Implement:

* Walking/running
* Facing and targeting opponents
* Collision
* Health or stamina
* Hit reactions
* Knockdowns
* Getting back up
* Basic AI
* Wrestling attacks
* Grappling
* Throws
* Environmental interaction
* Match feedback
* Camera behavior appropriate for a wrestling game

The player must have **five distinct wrestling moves**, triggered by:

**Y, U, L, O, P**

You may determine the exact moves, names, animations, damage, effects, ranges, and mechanics.

However, make the five moves meaningfully different. Include a mixture of attacks such as strikes, grapples, throws, signature attacks, movement attacks, or finishers rather than five variations of the same punch.

Whenever practical, tailor the move presentation to the selected wrestler's personality or apparent abilities while keeping them recognizable as wrestling moves.

Clearly display the controls and move names in the HUD.

## WRESTLING PRESENTATION

Character models should have convincing humanoid articulation and wrestling animations.

Prioritize:

* Proper scale
* Ground contact
* Readable silhouettes
* Convincing attack impacts
* Grapple alignment
* Character-to-character interaction
* Knockback
* Reactions
* Animation transitions
* Camera framing

Avoid characters visibly floating, sinking into the ring, clipping excessively through opponents, or performing attacks far away from their targets.

Moves involving two wrestlers should attempt to correctly position both characters before executing the animation.

## INTERACTIVE ENVIRONMENT

Make the arena more than scenery.

Include interactive objects and environmental opportunities wherever feasible, such as:

* Breakable objects
* Pick-up props
* Throwable objects
* Ring ropes
* Turnbuckles
* Objects beneath or beside the ring
* Arena decorations
* Character-themed weapons
* Physics objects
* Environmental hazards

Allow some wrestling action to spill outside the ring if practical.

The environment should encourage experimentation and chaotic free play.

## AUDIO AND VISUAL POLISH

Include procedural music and sound effects appropriate to the selected character's arena theme.

Use impactful audio for:

* Strikes
* Grapples

Add visual polish such as:

* Shadows

Avoid excessive darkness, blown-out lighting, or effects that obscure gameplay.

## OVERALL GOAL

This should feel like a small but surprisingly polished **character-driven 3D wrestling game**, not merely a technical demonstration.

The four wrestlers should feel distinct. Supplied reference characters should remain recognizable. The arena should strongly reflect whichever wrestler the player chooses, and changing characters should produce noticeably different arena themes, props, presentation, and atmosphere.

Prioritize **functionality as highly as visual detail**. Menus, character selection, controls, attacks, AI, collision, and match initialization must all work reliably.

Beyond these requirements, you have creative freedom.

**Go all out.**
