Create an ambitious, highly detailed, fully explorable **3D simulation of the world of Chrono Trigger across its major time periods**.

The central concept is a **single world viewed across radically different eras of history**. The player must be able to move through the world, select an era from a timeline, and watch the environment transform around their current geographic position.

## ERAS

Place a prominent timeline selector at the top of the screen with exactly these eras:

* **65,000,000 BC — Prehistory**
* **12,000 BC — Antiquity / Kingdom of Zeal**
* **600 AD — Middle Ages**
* **1000 AD — Present**
* **1999 AD — Day of Lavos**
* **2300 AD — Future**

Changing eras must produce a **major transformation of the world**, not merely change colors, lighting, textures, fog, or a few decorative objects.

This requirement is extremely important.

Each era should feel like a substantially different version of the same planet.

## CORE TIME-TRAVEL SYSTEM

The player's geographic position must persist when changing eras.

For example, if the player is standing at coordinates corresponding to a town in 1000 AD and switches to 65,000,000 BC, the town should disappear and be replaced by whatever prehistoric environment occupied approximately the same location.

Switching to 1999 AD might place the player among futuristic urban development at those coordinates.

Switching to 2300 AD should reveal the ruined aftermath of that civilization.

Where appropriate, preserve recognizable geographic relationships between eras so that the player can understand that these are different historical versions of the **same world**.

Era transitions should occur visually in front of the player whenever practical. Buildings can dissolve, collapse, construct themselves, become overgrown, or transform. Vegetation can grow or disappear. Roads can form. Technology can appear. Terrain can shift. Weather can change.

Avoid simply fading to black and loading another scene.

## 65,000,000 BC — PREHISTORY

Create a lush, dangerous primordial world dominated by nature.

Include:

* Dense prehistoric jungles
* Giant ferns and unusual vegetation
* Rocky mountains and cliffs
* Waterfalls and rivers
* Volcanic areas
* Primitive human settlements
* Reptite settlements and architecture
* Dinosaurs and other prehistoric creatures
* Primitive bridges and pathways
* Campfires
* Hunting areas
* The visual influence of locations such as Ioka Village and the Tyranno Lair
* Appropriate prehistoric ambient sounds

Civilization should occupy only tiny portions of the landscape.

The overwhelming impression should be of an ancient, wild planet.

## 12,000 BC — KINGDOM OF ZEAL

This should be one of the visual centerpieces of the simulation.

The surface world should be trapped in an extreme ice age with:

* Snow
* Glaciers
* Frozen mountains
* Snowstorms
* Small settlements inhabited by the Earthbound Ones
* Bleak, cold lighting and atmosphere

Above the clouds exists the magnificent **Kingdom of Zeal**.

Create multiple explorable floating islands featuring:

* Magical Zealian architecture
* Palaces
* Towns
* Bridges
* Gardens
* Water features
* Waterfalls falling from floating islands
* Magical machinery
* Glowing energy
* Scholars and citizens
* Zealian transportation or magical infrastructure
* The visual influence of locations such as Enhasa, Kajar, Zeal Palace and the Ocean Palace

Allow the player to fly upward from the frozen surface, pass through the cloud layer, and discover Zeal above.

The contrast between the miserable frozen surface and the beautiful magical civilization in the sky should be dramatic.

## 600 AD — MIDDLE AGES

Transform the world into a medieval civilization during the war between Guardia and the Mystics.

Include:

* Guardia Castle
* Medieval towns and villages
* Farms
* Forests
* Dirt roads
* Bridges
* Windmills where appropriate
* Soldiers
* Knights
* Merchants
* Peasants
* Medieval houses
* Fortifications
* War camps
* Mystic-controlled territory
* Zenan Bridge
* Denadoro Mountains
* Magus's Castle or a recognizable representation of it
* Environmental signs of an ongoing war

NPC clothing, architecture, props and activity should clearly communicate the medieval era.

## 1000 AD — PRESENT

The world should become peaceful and prosperous compared with 600 AD.

Maintain recognizable geographic continuity while substantially updating civilization.

Include:

* Guardia Castle
* Truce
* Porre
* Roads
* Bridges
* Farms
* Forests
* Inns
* Shops
* Residences
* Civilians
* Appropriate transportation
* The Millennial Fair at Leene Square
* Fair attractions, tents, banners and crowds
* Familiar environmental influences from Chrono Trigger's 1000 AD world

Locations that existed in 600 AD should visibly demonstrate approximately **400 years of development**.

Buildings should not simply be identical medieval buildings with different colors.

Architecture, settlement density, infrastructure and props should evolve.

## 1999 AD — DAY OF LAVOS

Do not portray 1999 AD as already destroyed.

Initially show the world **immediately before the Day of Lavos**.

This should depict Chrono Trigger's civilization at its technological height.

Extrapolate from the game's visual language to create a believable advanced civilization featuring:

* Large futuristic cities
* Skyscrapers
* Highways
* Advanced transportation
* Vehicles
* Aircraft
* Futuristic architecture
* Dense urban development
* Parks
* Industrial districts
* Energy infrastructure
* Electronic signage
* Large population centers
* Advanced technology
* Busy civilian life

This era should feel alive, technologically advanced and prosperous.

### DAY OF LAVOS EVENT

Include a special interactive **"Day of Lavos"** event.

When activated, recreate the catastrophe witnessed through the historical recording in Chrono Trigger.

The sequence should include:

* Warning alarms
* Environmental rumbling
* Increasing earthquakes
* Frightened civilians
* Lavos erupting from beneath the planet
* Massive eruptions
* Shockwaves
* Explosions
* Fires
* Destruction of cities
* Buildings collapsing
* Infrastructure failing
* Smoke filling the atmosphere
* Distant destruction visible across the horizon
* Dramatic sound effects

The player should be able to witness civilization being destroyed around them.

Make this one of the most impressive sequences in the simulation.

## 2300 AD — RUINED FUTURE

Show the long-term aftermath of the Day of Lavos.

The world should be bleak, ruined and barely habitable.

Include:

* Ruined futuristic cities
* Collapsed skyscrapers
* Broken highways
* Abandoned machinery
* Damaged domes
* Surviving inhabited domes
* Mutants
* Robots
* Hostile creatures
* Abandoned laboratories
* Destroyed transportation systems
* Dead vegetation
* Dust
* Polluted or altered environments
* Desolate terrain
* Small groups of struggling human survivors

Whenever possible, connect ruins to recognizable structures from 1999 AD.

For example:

A skyscraper visible in 1999 AD might exist as a shattered skeletal ruin in 2300 AD.

A major highway might become a broken elevated roadway.

A transportation center might become an abandoned ruin.

A populated district might become a damaged survival dome.

This historical continuity is extremely important.

The player should be able to explore 1999 AD, switch to 2300 AD at the same coordinates, and recognize what became of the surrounding civilization.

## WORLD DESIGN

Do not create six small disconnected dioramas.

Create the impression of a **complete Chrono Trigger world**.

Use a shared world coordinate system across every era.

It is acceptable to use lower-detail procedural terrain between important areas, but major locations should receive substantially higher detail.

Prioritize approximately **6–10 major hero locations per era** rather than making the entire world equally simplistic.

Use distant scenery, terrain, mountains, forests, oceans, settlements and atmospheric effects to make the world appear extensive.

Major landmarks should be visible from significant distances where appropriate.

## EXPLORATION

The player must be able to freely explore the 3D world.

Include:

* **WASD** — horizontal movement
* **Mouse** — camera/look
* **Q / E** — descend / ascend while flying
* **Shift** — increased movement speed
* Optional very-fast travel mode for crossing large distances

Provide two useful navigation scales:

### Exploration Mode

Human-scale movement for examining towns, buildings, characters and environmental details.

### World Mode

Fast aerial movement for traveling across continents and observing the world from high altitude.

Transitions between these modes should be smooth.

## CHARACTERS AND NPCS

Populate the eras with appropriate inhabitants.

NPCs should not merely stand motionless everywhere.

Where practical, include simple behaviors such as:

* Walking
* Talking
* Working
* Shopping
* Patrolling
* Guarding
* Farming
* Fleeing during danger
* Reacting to the Day of Lavos

Use simplified models if necessary, but give inhabitants recognizable silhouettes, outfits and behaviors appropriate to their era.

Characters and creatures should never spawn inside buildings, terrain, props, trees or other geometry.

## AUDIO

Sound design is important.

Include era-appropriate ambient sound such as:

* Wildlife
* Wind
* Rivers
* Waterfalls
* Crowds
* Medieval village ambience
* Magical Zealian ambience
* Machinery
* Futuristic city sounds
* Alarms
* Explosions
* Ruined-future wind and machinery

Audio should react to location and era.

Use generated Web Audio effects where external audio assets are unavailable.

## VISUAL QUALITY

Emphasis on detail is extremely important.

Use:

* High-quality lighting
* Shadows
* Atmospheric fog
* Sky effects
* Clouds
* Water
* Reflections where practical
* Particles
* Weather
* Vegetation
* Animated environmental elements
* Detailed architecture
* Appropriate materials
* Distant terrain
* Strong environmental composition

Lighting must remain readable.

Do not make night, underground or ruined environments so dark that scenery becomes difficult to see.

Avoid washed-out lighting.

## PERFORMANCE

Maintain good browser performance despite the scale of the project.

Use techniques such as:

* Instanced geometry
* Level of detail
* Distance culling
* Object pooling
* Procedural generation
* Simplified distant geometry
* Reusable materials
* Efficient particles
* Selective shadows

Spend rendering resources on things the player can actually see.

## USER INTERFACE

Create a polished interface inspired by Chrono Trigger without obstructing the world.

The timeline should remain easily accessible.

Display:

* Current era
* Current location or region
* Navigation controls
* Exploration / World mode
* Relevant special actions

For 1999 AD, prominently expose the **Day of Lavos** event control.

## CRITICAL REQUIREMENT — ERAS MUST BE SUBSTANTIALLY DIFFERENT

Do **NOT** implement the timeline primarily by changing:

* Sky colors
* Fog colors
* Building colors
* Lighting
* A few textures
* A few trees
* Small decorative props

Those elements may change, but they are supplementary.

Changing eras must substantially alter the actual scene geometry and content.

At minimum, era changes should affect:

* Terrain where appropriate
* Buildings
* Settlement locations
* Settlement density
* Architecture
* Roads
* Bridges
* Infrastructure
* Vegetation
* NPCs
* Creatures
* Clothing
* Technology
* Vehicles
* Props
* Landmarks
* Atmosphere
* Weather
* Audio
* Environmental activity

If a player changes from 65,000,000 BC to 12,000 BC to 600 AD to 1000 AD to 1999 AD to 2300 AD while remaining at the same coordinates, the differences should be immediately and dramatically obvious.

## PRIORITY

When forced to choose between **world size and environmental detail**, prioritize environmental detail.

When forced to choose between **large numbers of generic objects and fewer unique objects**, prioritize unique era-specific content.

When forced to choose between **perfect game accuracy and creating a convincing explorable representation of areas Chrono Trigger never directly depicts**, preserve established Chrono Trigger lore and visual identity while creatively extrapolating missing details.

The finished project should feel less like a timeline-controlled tech demo and more like a **small interactive 3D historical simulation of Chrono Trigger's planet**.

The defining experience should be:

**Find a place. Explore it. Change history. Watch the entire world around you become another age.**
